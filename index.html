Do I really never have to film again?
+
Once we train your avatar from one 10-minute filming session, it speaks any script we write. We recommend a refresh every 3–6 months. The weekly treadmill of recording yourself? Gone.
What does "qualified" actually mean?
+
Qualification criteria are customised for each doctor. In our experience a qualified patient is one that has: (1) a real condition with diagnosis or imaging, (2) no active contraindication for the treatment, and (3) intent to be treated within 60 days. Every lead is scored before reaching your calendar.
What are the HIPAA considerations?
+
Our standard platform configuration is not intended for the storage, transmission, or processing of Protected Health Information (PHI). As part of the standard implementation, we recommend that users do not enter patient records, diagnoses, treatment information, or other PHI into the system. For organizations that require the handling of PHI, we offer an enhanced healthcare configuration that includes the necessary HIPAA safeguards, execution of applicable Business Associate Agreements (BAAs), and additional compliance measures. This configuration is available as an optional service at an additional cost.
